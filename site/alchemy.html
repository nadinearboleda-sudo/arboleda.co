How Alchemy engagements work

Every engagement is scoped from the audit — fixed price, fixed timeline, no surprises.

01

Ops Automation Audit

2–3 weeks · fixed scope

We map 10–15 of your core finance and ops processes, score each for automation potential, effort and ROI, and identify the top five with estimated hours and dollars saved. You get a prioritised automation roadmap and an executive readout. The starting point for every client — low risk, fast, and the roadmap pays for itself.

02

Workflow Automation Build

4–10 weeks · scoped from the audit

We implement automation for your three to five priority processes — close checklist automation, AP approval flows, automated board reporting, expense workflows. Staff training and handover documentation included. Tooling is vendor-neutral: whatever fits your stack, never what earns a commission.

03

Automation Care Plan

Monthly · ongoing

We maintain and improve what we built: monthly health checks, break-fix, small enhancements and a quarterly ROI report — so the automations keep paying off as your tools and team change.
