How Aurora engagements work

Advisory grounded in your real data — not a slide deck about it.

01

Reporting & Metrics Architecture

4–8 weeks

We connect CRM, billing and product data to finance reporting and build your board pack, unit economics and forecast inputs as automated dashboards — in Tableau, Power BI or your existing stack. Single source of truth design: metric definitions, data lineage, ownership. For scale-ups prepping a raise: investors will ask, and the numbers need to reconcile.

02

AI Readiness & Governance

2 weeks · fixed scope

We assess data quality, system maturity and team capability for AI adoption, classify finance processes as safe-to-automate or not yet, and hand over a readiness scorecard, governance pack and 90-day action plan. A credible, practical answer when the board asks “what’s our AI strategy?” — without Big-4 prices.

03

Workforce & Capacity Planning

6–8 weeks

Scenario-based headcount and cost modelling — hire vs. automate vs. outsource — with supply/demand and capability gap analysis, and a cost-impact framework leadership can reuse every planning cycle.
