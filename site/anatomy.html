Anatomy · Foundations

Your business has a spine. Anatomy makes it explicit.

Anatomy is arboleda’s foundations practice. We map how your systems actually fit together — Xero, HubSpot, your PSA, Employment Hero — and connect them into one coherent architecture of systems, data and ownership. Everything else grows from here.

What Anatomy delivers

A clear picture of the stack you have, the stack you need, and the path between them.

  • Current-state systems map: ERP, billing, CRM, payroll, planning, BI
  • Target-state architecture with an integration plan
  • A unified data layer across your source systems
  • Metric definitions, data lineage and system ownership
  • Buyer’s-side vendor evaluation: RFPs, demo scorecards, negotiation
  • Migration sequencing that doesn’t disrupt what’s live

How Anatomy engagements work

Independent advice from the buyer’s side of the table — always.

01

Systems Map & Target Architecture

From the audit

We document every system touching finance and operations, where data lives, where it breaks, and who owns it — then design the target-state architecture and the integration plan to get there.

02

Systems Integration

Scoped per stack

We connect Xero, HubSpot, your PSA and Employment Hero through a unified integration layer — so every report and automation reads from one consistent source instead of a web of point-to-point syncs.

03

Vendor Selection
