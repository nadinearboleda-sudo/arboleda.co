What Anatomy delivers

A clear picture of the stack you have, the stack you need, and the path between them.

  • Current-state systems map: ERP, billing, CRM, payroll, planning, BI

  • A unified data layer across your source systems

Common questions

What does the systems map cover?

Every system touching finance and operations — ERP, billing, CRM, payroll, planning and BI — including where data lives, where it breaks and who owns it, plus a target-state architecture and the integration plan to get there.

Which systems do you integrate?

Xero, HubSpot, your PSA and Employment Hero, connected through a unified integration layer so every report and automation reads from one consistent source instead of point-to-point syncs.

How does vendor selection stay neutral?

We work strictly buyer's-side: requirements, shortlist, RFP support, demo scorecards and negotiation support. We are paid by you, never the vendor, and take no commissions.

How long does vendor selection take?

Typically six to ten weeks, covering requirements through to negotiation support — independent advice when you're facing a six-figure ERP or systems decision.

Know your own anatomy.

The systems review maps your current stack as part of the diagnostic — the natural first step before any architecture or vendor decision. Scoped within 48 hours.
